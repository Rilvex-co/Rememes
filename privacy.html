Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-22

Rememes ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data.

Information We Collect

We collect your email address, username, and profile information when you sign up. We also store content you upload (images/videos) and interactions such as likes, comments, and remixes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the core functionality of the app: displaying content, enabling social interactions, and maintaining your profile and XP.

Camera and Media Access

If you use the camera feature, we access your device camera only to capture images/videos within the app. We do not store camera footage without your action.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and storage. Their privacy policy applies to data stored on their platform.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at support@rememes.app.
